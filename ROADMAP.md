# Roadmap

## M0 — Foundation

Next.js application shell, typed domain model, source/lesson/error/TTS boundaries, environment contract, documentation, and test baseline.

## M1 — Discover & Reading

Implemented, awaiting acceptance: manual URL/text intake; CISA and Hugging Face RSS discovery; safe extraction/sanitisation; authentic reading; explicit sentence help; browser TTS; and temporary browser-local phrase persistence. No automatic lesson generation or Supabase persistence.

## M2 — AI Lesson Engine

Server-side lesson generation initiated by the learner. Add simplified-English support, optional Turkish help, prompts that isolate untrusted source data, and sanitised rendering.

## M3 — Writing & Error Memory

Writing attempts, focused feedback, stable personal error pattern keys, and imported ChatGPT Voice session reports.

## M4 — Review / Spaced Repetition

Phrase and error-pattern reviews with lightweight scheduling and active-vocabulary practice.

## M5 — Listening

Browser TTS controls first: sentence playback, speed, replay, and shadowing.

## M6 — Source Expansion

Additional RSS, Atom, manual URL, and source-specific adapters. No general crawler until its safety and maintenance costs are justified.

## M7 — Podcasts / Transcripts

Transcript extraction, future audio support, comprehension activities, and replay-based learning.

## M8 — Progress & IELTS-focused analytics

Progress snapshots and evidence-based skill indicators for B2 / IELTS 7 progression; never a fake score generator.
